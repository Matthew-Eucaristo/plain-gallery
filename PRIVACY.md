# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

Plain Gallery ("Gallery") is an open-source photo and video gallery for Android.

## What the app accesses
- **Photos and videos on your device**, to show, organize, share and delete them. Access is granted by you through Android's permission dialog and can be revoked at any time in system settings.
- **Location stored inside photos** (EXIF), only to show it in a photo's details when you ask for them.
- **Which app you picked when sharing**, stored on your device so it can be offered as a shortcut next time.
- **Text in your photos**, if "Search text in photos" is on (it is by default). The app reads the words in your screenshots and pictures so search can find them, entirely on your device with a model bundled in the app. Turning it off deletes what was read.
- **Names of installed apps**, only to show which app a screenshot was taken in (from its file name). Nothing about your apps is stored or sent.
- **Faces in your photos**, if "Group faces" is on (it is by default). The app finds faces and computes a numeric description of each one so photos of the same person can be grouped. This runs entirely on your device with models bundled in the app; face data and the names you give people never leave the device.

## What the app does not do
- It has **no internet permission**. It cannot send anything anywhere; you can verify this in the app's permissions or in the source code.
- No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
- Nothing is collected, sold or shared with the developer or anyone else.

## Data stored on your device
A small index of your media (file names, dates, sizes, folders) and your settings are kept in the app's private storage so the gallery opens quickly. Albums you make are kept there too (they only refer to your photos; files are not moved or copied). Text read from photos is kept there while "Search text in photos" is on. If face grouping is on, the face data (face positions, numeric face descriptions, groups and the names you give) is kept there too. Turning "Group faces" off in Settings deletes all face data immediately. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes everything. Photos you delete go to the Android system Trash, which empties itself after 30 days.

## Sharing
When you share, the files you chose are handed to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). If you choose a converted format, a temporary copy is created in the app's cache and removed automatically within an hour.
